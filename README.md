## The entry

> Monday morning is the only time my bed files for custody of me.

**Character count:** 63 (excluding the trailing newline; limit 1–200).

## Why it works

It lands on first read because it takes the most universal Monday feeling — not wanting to leave bed — and escalates it into a legal absurdity: the bed itself wants custody. One image, no setup to parse, no explanation needed, and the punchline ("files for custody") arrives at the very end. It is original, self-contained, and works as a single spoken line.
